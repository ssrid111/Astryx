# 🚀 Astryx

> **In Astryx, intelligence gets you closer to escape, but LUCK keeps you alive.**

Astryx is a **single-player 2D space survival and puzzle game** where a stranded astronaut must repair their damaged spacecraft, solve challenges, manage limited resources, and survive the interference of a mysterious cosmic entity.

But survival isn't determined by skill alone.

Before major challenges, the entity presents the player with the **Fate Roulette** — a mysterious wheel capable of granting valuable advantages or dangerous handicaps. Every spin can bring you one step closer to escape... or make survival even harder.

## 🎮 Gameplay

The player explores interconnected sections of a damaged spacecraft, interacting with the environment and completing puzzles to restore critical ship systems.

Throughout the game, players will:

- 🧩 Solve escape-room-inspired puzzles
- 🔧 Diagnose and repair damaged spaceship systems
- 🎰 Spin the **Fate Roulette** before major challenges
- 🎒 Collect and manage limited resources
- ⚠️ Survive unexpected hazards and equipment failures
- 🚀 Restore the spacecraft and escape the entity

## 🎰 Fate Roulette

The **Fate Roulette** is one of Astryx's core gameplay mechanics.

Before certain challenges, the mysterious entity allows the player to spin the wheel. The result can dramatically change the conditions of the upcoming challenge.

### Possible Advantages

- Temporary immunity from hazards
- Additional tools or repair components
- Extra challenge time
- Puzzle hints
- Additional survival resources

### Possible Disadvantages

- Reduced challenge time
- Loss of resources
- Temporary equipment failure
- Reduced visibility
- Additional obstacles

Every spin changes the conditions of survival. Players must adapt to whatever fate gives them.

## 🛰️ The Mission

Your spacecraft is critically damaged.

**Power. Navigation. Communications. Life Support. Propulsion.**

Each system must be restored by exploring the ship and completing challenges. Repair enough of the spacecraft to regain control and escape before the entity's game becomes impossible to survive.

**Solve. Spin. Survive. Escape.**

## 🌌 Setting

Astryx takes place aboard a damaged spacecraft stranded in deep space.

The player is a lone astronaut attempting to return home while an unknown entity watches from somewhere beyond the ship. Rather than directly attacking the astronaut, the entity manipulates their surroundings and turns their struggle for survival into a game.

Sometimes it helps.

Sometimes it doesn't.

And you never know what it will do next.

## 🎨 Art & Design

Astryx uses a **2D hand-drawn visual style** for its environments, characters, objects, puzzles, and other game elements.

The game is being developed using a **3D game engine configured for 2D gameplay**, allowing the team to combine hand-drawn assets with the flexibility of a 3D development environment.
